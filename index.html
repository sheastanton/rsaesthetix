About Us

RS Aesthetix is a premium mobile detailing service based in Charlotte, NC. We bring luxury detailing directly to you with a focus on clean finishes, spotless interiors, glossy exterior results, and a seamless client experience.

Whether your vehicle needs a full reset, routine maintenance, pet hair removal, paint enhancement, or a complete interior and exterior detail, our goal is to deliver professional results with attention to every surface. At RS Aesthetix, convenience, quality, and customer satisfaction are our top priorities -- because your vehicle deserves nothing less than perfection.

Policies

Booking

Appointments must be scheduled through our booking page. Please choose the service that best matches your vehicle's condition. By booking with us, you agree to our policies and terms.

Travel Area

Travel is included within the I-485 loop. Additional travel fees may apply for appointments outside the standard service area.

Vehicle Condition

Final pricing will vary depending on vehicle size, condition, stains, pet hair, or specialty add-on services. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us before booking.

Weather

Mobile appointments may be rescheduled for heavy rain, storms, unsafe conditions, or weather that affects service quality.

Cancellation Policy

A 20% non-refundable deposit is required to secure an appointment. Cancellations made within 48 hours of the appointment will result in a 50% fee. Missing an appointment without notice will result in a 100% charge. Contact us ASAP if an emergency arises.
